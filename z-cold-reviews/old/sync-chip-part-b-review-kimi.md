### Solid Sections (Briefly Noted)

- **B1's rejection of `reconcile()` for freshness checks** is correct; keeping reads side-effect-free is the right call.
- **B2's reuse of the existing `onBehind` primitive** for conflict resolution is sound and avoids inventing new UX.
- **The three-branch split** is well-justified by the standalone-file architecture and the prior review's convergence on incremental mergeability.
- **Explicit `esc()` coverage for chip surfaces** is correctly scoped to a real trust boundary.

The `pendingEdit` / `getSyncState()` circular dependency (finding 1) and the missing `behind` storage schema (finding 2) would likely have been discovered only during implementation or in a broken B1 branch, costing a rewrite cycle.
